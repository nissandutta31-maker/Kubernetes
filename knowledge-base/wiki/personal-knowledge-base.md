# Personal Knowledge Base

> A personal knowledge base (PKB) is a curated collection of small, linked notes that
> externalizes your thinking so it compounds over time instead of being re-derived.

## Context
This whole `knowledge-base/` is a PKB. Its mechanics are codified in the schema ([[CLAUDE]])
and its capture inbox is [[raw/README]]. The design borrows from Zettelkasten (atomic,
addressable notes), "evergreen notes" (notes that improve over time), and the
capture-vs-distill split popularized by modern note-taking systems.

## Key points
- **Atomic notes:** one idea per page. Small notes are easier to reuse and link than essays.
- **Links over hierarchy:** structure emerges from `[[wiki-links]]`, not from deep folders.
  A [[index]] Map of Content is the navigable surface on top of the link graph.
- **Capture ≠ distill:** raw sources are stored verbatim and immutably in `raw/`; thinking
  and synthesis happen later in `wiki/`. This keeps capture frictionless.
- **Compounding:** value comes from connections between notes, so every new note should link
  to existing ones.
